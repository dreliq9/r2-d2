## Notes on what the formula is doing

- **Pilot package nearly doubled** (12→23) — Luke's leader text wants Vehicles + Pilots, formula amplified accordingly. Half the deck has Piloting.
- **Real top end** — Tantive IV, Medical Frigate, Quasar TIE Carrier, Longbeam Cruiser, Razor Crest. r1 was hard-capped at 4-cost; r2 reaches 7-cost while keeping avg below 3.0.
- **Heroism character chassis** — Han Solo, Hera Syndulla, Poe Dameron, Red Leader, Admiral Yularen, alt-Luke. POWER scoring pulled high-rarity strong-text characters.
- **Sabine's Masterpiece** — your "genuinely good" call validated again.
- **22 off-aspect cards** — these survived the −25 penalty because their interaction value is real:
  - **Vehicle/Pilot payoffs**: Hera, Paige, Academy Graduate, Trace, Astromech Pilot, Yularen — each genuinely pays off the Vehicle package
  - **Vehicle event tutors only exist in Command**: Prepare For Takeoff, Focus Fire, Protect the Pod, Eject — restricting events to on-aspect would gut this slot
  - **Genuine power picks**: Mist Hunter, Red Leader, Luke (You Still With Me?), Jarek Yeager — strong cards whose synergy + power outweigh the +2 resource off-aspect cost
- **Interaction density 350.7** is tribal-tier (vs ~100 typical for goodstuff Twin Suns) — confirms the deck is leaning hard into its Vehicle/Pilot synergy
