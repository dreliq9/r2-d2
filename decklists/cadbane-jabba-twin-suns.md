# Cad Bane / Jabba the Hutt — Twin Suns
**Format:** Twin Suns | **Aspects:** Cunning, Villainy, Command | **Synergy:** 92 | **Avg Cost:** 3.5

## Strategy
Aggro/tempo bounty hunter deck. Every unit you play is Underworld, triggering Cad Bane's ping (1 damage undeployed, 2 deployed). Jabba gives enemies Bounties — when your bounty hunters kill them, you get cost reductions and resource ramps. Flood the board with cheap bodies, each one dealing incremental removal through Cad's ability. 67 Underworld-trait cards, 20 Bounty Hunters.

This is the opposite of the Qui-Gon builds: no combo setup, no engine to assemble. Just play units, ping things, attack. Lowest skill floor, highest aggression.
