## Notes

- Rose's leader text (heal 2 from a Vehicle that attacked this phase) is a real engine here — 39 Vehicle units means she rarely has a dead turn. Sentinel 7 + Restore 4 keep wide boards alive long enough to claim the heal.
- Paige + Rose Tico is the flavor anchor (sisters). Both feed the Resistance/Vehicle subplan.
- Trades raw aggro speed for grind — the curve climbs into Capital Ships (Tantive IV, Radiant VII, CR90, Longbeam, Medical Frigate) which the Ackbar build doesn't touch.
- Removal density holds (30 vs Ackbar's 32) so interaction is preserved.
- Main risk: 11 events vs Ackbar's 17 — fewer reactive answers, more reliance on units doing the work.
