# Grand Admiral Thrawn / Emperor Palpatine — Twin Suns
**Format:** Twin Suns | **Aspects:** Vigilance, Villainy, Command | **Synergy:** TBD | **Avg Cost:** ~3.4

## Strategy
Imperial control built around **Annihilator, Tagge's Flagship** as the win condition. JTL Thrawn (*…How Unfortunate*) is the engine — every *When Defeated* trigger fires twice. Annihilator's WD already kills a unit and exiles all copies of its name from hand+deck; doubled by Thrawn, it kills two units and exiles two names. If Annihilator itself dies, Thrawn doubles its WD again — up to four kill-and-exile events off one 11-drop.

Palpatine - Galactic Ruler partners as the second Villainy leader, adding Command access (ramp + draw) and his own deployed 9/9 body with a defeat-a-unit WD that Thrawn also doubles.

Stall the early game with cheap Villainy units that have *When Defeated* triggers (Krennic, Wartime Trade Official, Inferno Four), ramp into Chimaera turn 5, Malevolence/Executor turn 6–7, Annihilator turn 7–8. Trade aggressively because every death is value under Thrawn.

## ⚠️ Caveats — read before buying

1. **Aspect verification needed.** This deck uses Vigilance/Villainy/Command (Thrawn JTL + Palpatine GR). The events list was lifted from a Premier mono-Thrawn (Vig/Vil only) reference. Several events (Force Lightning, Shatterpoint, Superlaser Blast) may be Aggression — they'd cost +2 resources off-aspect under this leader pair. Verify each event's aspect on swudb before locking in the list.

2. **Premier → Twin Suns conversion isn't 1:1.** The reference lists are 50-card Premier decks. This deck needs 80 singletons. The "filler" section above is rough — uses your Boba spotlight cards as Imperial-themed filler, but a real brew would replace them with stronger Vig/Vil units (Death Trooper Squad, ISB Agent, Star Destroyer fleet pieces, more droid army).

3. **Leader pairing is theoretical.** The only proven competitive Twin Suns Thrawn list found in databases pairs him with **Maul** ("Shadow Lord" archetype, Aggression/Villainy). PalpThrawn was *named* in research but not confirmed with a list. If Maul leader is available in your collection, that's the battle-tested pairing — and it shifts aspects to Vig/Vil/Agg, which would actually validate Force Lightning / Shatterpoint / Superlaser Blast.

4. **Annihilator dependency.** This deck lives or dies on whether you pull (or buy) Annihilator. If JTL box doesn't drop one, single it before sleeving up.
